About Giftlio

Every card sold on Giftlio goes through admin review before it's ever listed. Payments run through Stripe, the same infrastructure used by major NZ retailers — Giftlio never sees or stores your card details.

As an NZ-registered business, Giftlio is bound by the Consumer Guarantees Act and Fair Trading Act, the same as any NZ retailer. A real person reads every support message.

How it started

Giftlio started with a drawer full of gift cards I was never going to use.

A birthday voucher here, a leftover balance there — cards from stores I don't shop at, sitting forgotten while I paid full price everywhere else. I figured I couldn't be the only one. Kiwis are sitting on millions of dollars in unused gift card balances, and there was no simple, trustworthy way to turn that into cash — or to buy discounted cards from people in exactly the same position.

So I built Giftlio myself, from Auckland, to fix that. Every card that goes through it gets reviewed by hand before it's ever listed — not because a system told me to, but because I'd want that if I were buying. Payments run through Stripe rather than anything I've built myself, because your card details are not something I want to be responsible for storing.

Why I'm building this in the open

Giftlio is early. It's a small, NZ-owned operation, not a big company — which means when something needs fixing, I'm the one who fixes it, and when you email support, you're emailing me. I'd rather be upfront about being new and earn trust properly than pretend to be bigger than I am.

What I'm aiming for

A marketplace New Zealanders actually trust with their money — for buyers who want a fair discount without wondering if the card is real, and for sellers who want cash for a card they were never going to use, without the hassle of finding a buyer themselves.

— Mohammed, Founder

How Every Submission Is Reviewed

We never list a card without reviewing it first -- this is what actually happens behind the scenes.

1. Seller submits card details

Card number, current balance, and a receipt or photo as evidence -- encrypted the moment it reaches us.

2. A real person reviews it

A card is never listed for sale until this review is complete. If something looks wrong, it's rejected, not listed.

Sell Your Gift Card

Turn a card you're never going to use into cash.

Giftlio is a New Zealand marketplace where people sell gift cards they don't want and buy them at a discount from people who do. If you've got a balance sitting on a card for a shop you never visit, you can submit it here and list it for sale.

Giftlio is currently testing its service with a limited number of New Zealand sellers and buyers. Retailer support, availability, verification times, and payout timing may be limited during the pilot.

Who can submit a card

Any Giftlio account holder can submit a gift card for one of our supported retailers, provided the card is legally theirs to sell and the balance is accurate. You'll need to create a free account, or log in, before submitting.

Supported retailers

What you'll need to submit

  • The retailer, card value, and current balance
  • The card's expiry date
  • The card number, and PIN if the card has one
  • A receipt or a photo of the card as evidence -- at least one is required
  • Your own asking price -- on the Marketplace you set the price, and Giftlio takes a 10% commission once it sells

Review

1. You submit the details

Card number, balance, and a receipt or photo -- encrypted the moment it reaches us.

2. We review it

A Giftlio admin looks it over and decides whether to approve or reject it before it's listed for sale.

3. It goes live, or it doesn't

If everything checks out, it's listed at your price. If not, it's rejected rather than listed.

Common reasons a submission is rejected

Something about the balance or details didn't check out during admin review.
No receipt or card photo was provided, and one is required for this retailer.
Cards past their expiry date can't be resold.
During the pilot, a retailer can be temporarily paused for submissions -- your card can be resubmitted once it reopens.

If a submission isn't approved, we'll email you the reason.

Getting paid

Once your card sells, payout is released 48 hours after it's delivered to the buyer, by bank transfer.

Giftlio is a marketplace where New Zealanders sell gift cards they're not going to use. As a buyer, you get to shop at those retailers for less than the card's face value.

How the discount works

Every card is listed by a real seller who sets their own asking price -- so the discount you see on a listing reflects what they were willing to accept for it, not a fixed markdown Giftlio applies. Prices and discounts vary by listing.

How inventory becomes available

A card only becomes available to buy once it's passed admin review. There's no fixed stock -- what's listed depends on what sellers have submitted and had approved, so availability can be limited, especially during the pilot.

What review means

Before a card is listed for sale, a Giftlio admin reviews the submission and decides whether to approve or reject it -- not an automated guess.

Partner With Giftlio

For clubs, schools, charities, employers, and event organisers sitting on surplus vouchers.

If your organisation ends up with gift cards or vouchers that go unused -- leftover prizes, sponsorship perks, staff gifts nobody claimed -- Giftlio's marketplace gives you a way to turn them into cash instead of letting them expire in a drawer.

Giftlio is currently testing its service with a limited number of New Zealand sellers and buyers. Retailer support, availability, verification times, and payout timing may be limited during the pilot.

Who we're looking to work with

  • Sports clubs and community groups with unused sponsor or prize vouchers
  • Schools and PTAs with leftover fundraising or event vouchers
  • Charities holding donated gift cards they can't use directly
  • Employers with unclaimed staff reward or gift cards
  • Event organisers with surplus prize or giveaway vouchers

What the pilot involves

During the pilot, an organisation works through Giftlio the same way an individual seller does: create an account, submit each voucher's details, and it goes through the same admin review as any other submission before it's listed for sale. There's no separate bulk-upload process yet -- each card is submitted individually.

Support and availability

We're taking on a limited number of organisations during the pilot, and support is currently handled directly by our small team rather than a dedicated partnerships desk. Response times and retailer coverage may vary while we're still testing.
