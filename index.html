About Giftlio

Giftlio started with a drawer full of gift cards I was never going to use.

A birthday voucher here, a leftover balance there — cards from stores I don't shop at, sitting forgotten while I paid full price everywhere else. I figured I couldn't be the only one. Kiwis are sitting on millions of dollars in unused gift card balances, and there was no simple, trustworthy way to turn that into cash — or to buy discounted cards from people in exactly the same position.

So I built Giftlio myself, from Auckland, to fix that. Every card that goes through it is checked by hand against the retailer's own balance tool before it's ever listed — not because a system told me to, but because I'd want that if I were buying. Payments run through Stripe rather than anything I've built myself, because your card details are not something I want to be responsible for storing.

Why I'm building this in the open

Giftlio is early. It's a small, NZ-owned operation, not a big company — which means when something needs fixing, I'm the one who fixes it, and when you email support, you're emailing me. I'd rather be upfront about being new and earn trust properly than pretend to be bigger than I am.

What I'm aiming for

A marketplace New Zealanders actually trust with their money — for buyers who want a fair discount without wondering if the card is real, and for sellers who want cash for a card they were never going to use, without the hassle of finding a buyer themselves.

— Mohammed, Founder
