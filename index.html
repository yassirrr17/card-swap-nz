Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

CardSwap NZ (we, us, our) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal: name, email, phone number
  • Payment: card details processed by Stripe (we do not store full card numbers)
  • Gift Card Data: card numbers, PINs, balances, expiry dates
  • Usage: pages visited, searches made, device information

3. How We Use Information

  • To process transactions and deliver gift cards
  • To verify gift card balances
  • To communicate about orders and support
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage

Data stored on secure servers. Gift card details encrypted. NZ-based hosting where possible.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Shared only with: Stripe (payments), email service (communications). Legal requirements: may disclose if required by NZ law.

6. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion (with limitations for legal/transaction records)

Contact: privacy@cardswap.nz

7. Cookies

Essential cookies for site functionality. No tracking cookies for advertising.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes posted on this page.

Terms and Conditions

Last updated: July 2026

1. Agreement

By using CardSwap NZ, you agree to these terms. If you disagree, do not use our service.

2. Marketplace Rules

  • CardSwap NZ is a platform connecting buyers and sellers of gift cards
  • We do not own the gift cards listed
  • All transactions are between users; we facilitate payment and verification

3. Buyer Terms

  • Buyers must be 18 years or older
  • Gift card details delivered via email within 24 hours of purchase
  • Buyers must verify card balance within 24 hours of receipt
  • Report issues immediately via support email
  • All sales are final after card details are delivered, unless balance is incorrect

4. Seller Terms

  • Sellers must legally own all gift cards listed
  • Must provide accurate balance information
  • Stolen or fraudulent cards are prohibited and will result in account suspension
  • Sellers receive 85% of selling price; platform retains 15% service fee
  • Payouts processed within 3-5 business days after card sale

5. Prohibited Activity

  • Selling stolen or fraudulently obtained gift cards
  • Creating multiple accounts to manipulate the marketplace
  • Attempting to circumvent platform fees
  • Harassment of other users

6. Account Suspension

We reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts for violations. Suspended accounts forfeit pending payouts. Appeals can be made via support email.

7. Limitation of Liability

We verify card balances to the best of our ability but are not responsible for retailer policy changes. We are not liable for lost profits or indirect damages. Maximum liability limited to the transaction amount.

8. Refund Policy

No refunds after gift card details are delivered, unless the balance is proven incorrect. Incorrect balance claims must be submitted within 48 hours with evidence. Refunds processed to original payment method within 5-10 business days.

9. Governing Law

These terms governed by New Zealand law. Disputes resolved in NZ courts.

10. Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@cardswap.nz
